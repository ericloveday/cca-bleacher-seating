How This App Works

Seating Charts

Create one entry per event/assembly (+ New Chart). Each chart has its own roster and seating — switching the dropdown swaps everything at once. Rename Chart changes the name only. Delete Chart removes it entirely, including its roster and seating — this can't be undone. Set Your Name once — it's saved automatically and appears on printed PDFs.

Adding students

  • Type a name and pick M/F/- (or leave blank), then click + Add Student (or press Enter).
  • CSV upload format: Last, First, Gender(M/F) — one student per line. A header row is detected and skipped automatically.
  • Click the (M)/(F)/(-) badge on any unassigned student's chip to change their gender anytime.
  • Click a student's name to rename them — this doesn't move or unseat them.
  • Clear Roster removes every student at once (and clears seating too).

Two Views

  • 👁 Clean View (default) — a tidy, read-only look at the chart. Great for glancing at the layout or before printing.
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Arrange Students — shows the roster tools. Each filled seat shows lock/undo icons, and you can drag students from Unassigned onto open seats. The bleacher rows/sections themselves never move.

Seating students

  • Drag a student chip from Unassigned onto any open seat.
  • 📌 Place Students: fills every unlocked seat with unlocked students, using the Order dropdown (Random, or Alphabetical by first/last name).
  • Alternate gender checkbox: interleaves boy/girl in seat order instead of pure random. Only applies when Order is Random.
  • Clear Seats: sends everyone back to Unassigned.

Seat icons (in Arrange Students mode)

  • 🔒 / 🔓 click to lock/unlock a seat. Locked seats — occupied or empty — are skipped by Place Students and protected from drag-and-drop.
  • The undo arrow on a filled, unlocked seat instantly sends that student back to Unassigned.
  • 🔓 Clear All Locks unlocks every seat at once without touching who's seated.
IEP / 504 seating: place the student in the seat they need, click 🔒 to lock it, then hit 📌 Place Students as usual — everyone else fills in around them.

Exporting

📄 Export PDF opens a picker for Landscape, Portrait, or Auto (Best Fit). It adds the chart name, your name, and the date/time as the header.
